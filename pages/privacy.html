1. Introduction

Kasturii Roadways Private Limited ("Kasturii Roadways", "we", "our" or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and safeguard information when you use our website or services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name, mobile number and email address when you contact us or submit our contact form
  • Booking details when you book through our platform or via WhatsApp
  • Usage data through analytics tools (browser type, pages visited, time spent)
  • Location data (only if you request route navigation assistance)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and confirm ticket bookings
  • To respond to customer support enquiries
  • To send booking confirmations and journey updates
  • To improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share your information with:

  • Third-party booking platforms (redBus, MakeMyTrip) as required to process your booking
  • Payment processors to complete transactions
  • Legal authorities when required by law

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@kastuuriroadways.in.

7. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve user experience. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact: support@kastuuriroadways.in
