Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

← Back to Home

1. General Policy

Kasturii Roadways Private Limited aims to make cancellations and refunds as straightforward as possible. The cancellation and refund terms depend on the platform through which you booked your ticket.

2. Cancellations via redBus or MakeMyTrip

If you booked through redBus or MakeMyTrip, cancellation and refund are governed by their respective policies. Please refer to the platform's cancellation page or contact their customer support for assistance.

3. Cancellations via WhatsApp / Direct Booking

The following schedule applies to direct bookings made via WhatsApp or our support team:

Cancellation Before Departure Refund Amount
More than 48 hours100% refund (less processing fee)
24 – 48 hours75% refund
12 – 24 hours50% refund
Less than 12 hoursNo refund
No-showNo refund

4. How to Request Cancellation

  • WhatsApp: Send a message to our support team with your booking details
  • Email: support@kastuuriroadways.in
  • Phone: Call our customer support number during office hours

5. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days. Refunds are made to the original payment method used at the time of booking.

6. Cancellations by Kasturii Roadways

If a trip is cancelled by Kasturii Roadways due to operational reasons, passengers will receive a full refund or the option to rebook on an alternative service without additional charge.

7. Contact

For cancellation queries: support@kastuuriroadways.in
